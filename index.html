BPS Bali

Why the market is worth a file this careful

Demand is published. The failure is the file.

Direct foreign arrivals to Bali were 6.28 million in 2019. They were 6.95 million in 2025, the highest year in the BPS series, 9.72% above 2024. Australia was 23.44% of that 2025 total. Domestic trips in the same year were 26.6 million.

A brochure treats that demand as a yield. The philosophy on this page is the opposite. Arrivals explain why a well-run asset in this corridor has a guest. They do not explain whether the land can legally be built, whether the building can legally be used, or who is still accountable in year five. That is the stack.

Read the method

The process

Source. Permit. Build. Stay.

Six checks, in the order the statute forces. Skip one and the arrival number does not save the asset.

01

Source. The land has to be allowed to be the building.

Before a business licence treats a site as real, PP 28 of 2025 asks for KKPR: confirmation that the activity matches the spatial plan. Bali’s RDTR and RTRW are what that check is read against. A contour with the wrong zone is how a building later meets a demolition order. PP 16 of 2021 already lists demolition as an enforcement tool, and the 18 September call recorded that illegal builds on the island have been taken down.

This is why the homepage leads with place, not a render. The Carrd names Bali, Lombok, and the North Bali airport belt. The strategy names Tabanan as the window. The company page says Tabanan and the Carrd says Seminyak. The file picks one address, then proves the zone, before anyone talks about a view.

02

Structure. PT PMA, because freehold is not on offer.

Law 5 of 1960, the Basic Agrarian Law, keeps Hak Milik with Indonesian citizens. A foreign allocator does not become a freeholder by putting an Indonesian name on the certificate. That nominee pattern is the failure the market still sells around, often beside a Pondok Wisata licence that covers guests and does not cure title.

The words already in public are a PT PMA, the foreign-investment company, and a lease put through an Indonesian notary. The company is the entity that can hold a right to build. The notarial deed is the instrument a court can read. What those two actually protect, and what they do not, waits on the legal memo. Until that memo is signed, this page will not call the structure risk-free.

03

Permit. PBG before the steel is ordered.

Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung replaced the old IMB. Under PP 16 of 2021 it is the approval to build, alter, or maintain against the technical standards. For an operator, PP 28 of 2025 makes PBG a basic requirement and requires it before construction starts. The review is where site coverage, floor area, green area, and setbacks are tested, and where the structure has to meet the standards, including seismic design.

Kayra sits on the homepage because the company that will build the drawing is the company that has to live with what the permit allows. A builder hired after the sale can treat the PBG as someone else’s document. Keystone Kayra Construction is the build arm on Christopher’s profile. KHC holds product, sequence, and cost.

04

Specification. The wall is the line the yield table hides.

The code does not mandate a brand of wall. It mandates that the drawn building can pass the technical standards, and later the certificate of use. The 18 September call described the Bali default as FC column and Hebel, chosen because it is quick, and moisture-prone in a monsoon climate. The same call put the renovation at about year five, and the KHC specification at steel stud, intended for 15 to 20 years.

The 4 + 1 on the homepage is that choice made numerical. Two steel-stud factories, a priority queue, about four large new builds and one development of 8 to 10 units a year. Management can scale. The wall cannot pretend to. Specifying it before sale is how the operator stops the easy detail becoming the investor’s renovation. Christopher is still writing this in his own sentences. Until that note lands, this is the call, not a finished specification.

05

Function. SLF is the moment the building may earn.

Sertifikat Laik Fungsi, in PP 16 of 2021, is the certificate that the building is fit to be used. PP 28 of 2025 requires it before a business building is put to use. A PBG is not an SLF. A finished render without the certificate is not an operating asset, and the certificate has to be renewed.

This is why “stay” is a verb and not a hospitality slogan. The handover model, the pattern named against Kiss Developments on the call, leaves the investor to obtain, renew, and defend that certificate after the builder has gone. KHC’s contrast is that management remains with the name that sold the position. The percent is not settled in our own papers: 25% in the strategy file, 20% plus a 5% sinking fund on the public profile. One of those becomes the sentence. Not both, and not yet.

06

Stay. The exit is written at entry.

A lease is a term. Who holds it, in which currency the economics are reported, and who can sell, belong in the same file as the KKPR and the PBG. The three rungs further down are how capital comes in. They do not replace this sequence, and they do not turn a cohort ticket into a villa.

One operator is the point of the homepage because steps 01 to 06 are usually four contracts and then nobody. Source, permit, wall, certificate, management, exit. The same name, or the seams open exactly where the statute is strictest.

Questions the file should answer

Is the title a structure, or a promise?

PT PMA and a notarised leasehold are the words already in public. What they protect, and what they do not, waits on the legal memo.

Who is still there in year five?

Keystone Kayra builds. KHC holds product, sequence, and cost, then stays for management. That is the opposite of a handover.

Why not a villa from $540k and a month of personal use?

That is a holiday product with a yield glued on. KHC’s door is a cohort, then a seed ticket, then a larger allocation, for people who want the stack rather than a second house.

Where do the return figures come from?

Arrival totals are BPS Bali. Corridor land rates on this draft are from the category brief and are not cleared. The internal model, 13.6% IRR and 1.76× base MOIC, is on the areas page and is not cleared.

Why is a finished villa not proof the process was followed?

Occupation needs an SLF. Construction needs a PBG. The land use needs a KKPR. A Pondok Wisata licence and a nominee certificate do not stand in for those three. Buildings without them have been demolished.
