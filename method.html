Specification before sale

The wall is the investment.

Bali’s default is FC column and Hebel block, used because it is easy. Hebel is moisture-prone in a monsoon climate. KHC specifies steel stud modular construction.

Market default

Renovate around year five.

Year 0 Render, handover, the builder leaves.
Year 2 Moisture in the block. The snag list grows.
Year 5 A renovation the yield table never showed.
KHC specification

A 15 to 20 year frame.

Spec Steel stud. Two factories with a priority queue, as described on the call.
File Zoning, PPG, SLS, PCPMA. Illegal builds have been demolished. The file is the product.
Stay Modestly higher upfront cost. Longevity per dollar, not a cheaper wall.

Christopher is writing this methodology in his own sentences. Until that note lands, this page uses the 18 September call and the marketing strategy record. Do not treat it as his finished specification.

Supply

Named, not generic.

Tiles, windows, aluminium, stairs, electrical, and plumbing sourced through a China line he already runs. Savings claims on his profile stay off this page until the file exists.

Capacity

Four, plus one.

About four large new builds and one development of 8 to 10 units a year, capped by the steel supplier. Management can scale. Construction cannot pretend to.

Contrast

No handover.

The call’s comparison is a developer who buys, designs, and leaves. KHC’s management share is the reason the operator is still in the story.
