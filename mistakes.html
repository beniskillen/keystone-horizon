The Bali property nightmare is a missing file.

Arrivals are at a record. Star hotels in Bali were the fullest in Indonesia in 2025. The failures that reach an Australian investor are rarely “Bali did not have guests.” They are title, permit, specification, and a builder who has already left.

01

Buying freehold in someone else’s name.

Law 5 of 1960 keeps Hak Milik with Indonesian citizens. A nominee arrangement borrows a right the statute does not give a foreign allocator. It can look clean in a scan. It is the structure that does not survive a dispute, a death, or a serious buyer’s notary.

The public KHC words are a PT PMA and a notarised lease. What those protect still waits on counsel. The mistake is treating “the local partner’s certificate” as ownership.

02

A Pondok Wisata licence where a PBG should be.

A tourism-cottage licence lets a house take guests. It does not replace Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung, and it does not replace Sertifikat Laik Fungsi. PP 16 of 2021 and, for a business operator, PP 28 of 2025, split those jobs: approval before you build, certificate before you use the building.

Illegal builds on Bali have been demolished. A finished villa is not evidence the sequence was followed.

03

Paying for a render.

Off-plan is where the picture and the budget part company. The drawing shows a finish the contract does not specify. The contractor’s bill of quantities is written to fit the number, then the variations arrive. The protection is a specification before sale, an independent quantity check, and payments tied to progress, not a lump sum against a sunset image.

Keystone Kayra is on the homepage because the build arm and the permit have to be the same side of the table. Christopher is still writing the wall specification in his own words. Until that note lands, the steel-stud line is the 18 September call, not a finished standard.

04

Buying the subway, the toll, or the financial centre.

The urban subway had a groundbreaking in September 2024. In February 2026 the Kuta site still showed no follow-on works. The Pekutatan–Mengwi toll is aimed at a late-2026 tender and 2027 works, on a shortened 42 km scheme. KEK Kura Kura Bali is a designated zone under PP 23 of 2023. An international financial centre in Bali is being prepared. It had not been designated as of May 2026.

Benoa’s yacht marina is the one of these that is actually open: 75 yachts from July 2026. A sales line that treats all five as “coming infrastructure, therefore yield” is how people overpay for a zone that cannot yet be let.

05

One occupancy number for every product.

BPS put star-hotel room occupancy in Bali at 61.02% in 2025, the highest province, against 49.30% for Indonesia. Airbtics, for November 2024 to October 2025, put median occupancy on Bali’s active Airbnb-style listings at 65%, with average annual revenue around IDR 326 million and monthly revenue down 6.13% while arrivals rose. Those are different markets. A brochure that prints 13% net, or a Canggu villa occupancy in the mid-70s, without saying which stock and which costs, is selling a blend.

KHC does not publish a cleared yield. The internal model stays off this page until the substantiation file exists. The argument is the spread: hotels are full enough to prove guests; undifferentiated villas are competing with tens of thousands of listings.

06

Three contracts, then nobody.

Land from one party, a build from another, management from a third, and an exit that was never written down. That is the handover pattern. The investor discovers the SLF renewal, the moisture in the wall, and the lease with no priced extension after the people who sold the picture have gone.

The stack is the answer KHC is willing to be judged on: source, permit, specification, certificate, stay, and a written way out, under one name. The management percent is still unresolved between the strategy file and the public profile. That conflict is printed, not smoothed over.
