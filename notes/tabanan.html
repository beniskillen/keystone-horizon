Decide · Place

A window is not a road.

Tabanan is the place the strategy names. The toll that would change the drive from the west is aimed at a late-2026 tender. Those two facts should not be folded together.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

Why this coast is in the file

The established streets, Canggu through Uluwatu, are where the launches already are. Tabanan is the regency beside them: rice at Jatiluwih, coast at the west, still inside an island that just posted 6.95 million foreign arrivals and 5.82% economic growth. The strategy calls it the window because the land has not been through the same launch cycle. That is a qualitative claim. The quantitative version, a per-square-metre gap versus Canggu, sits in the internal file without a source row. It is not repeated here.

The address still has to be chosen once. Company page, Carrd, and strategy have not always said the same place. A window that cannot point at a title is a mood.

The road people will mention

The Gilimanuk–Mengwi scheme was cut back. The toll that remains is about 42 kilometres, Pekutatan to Mengwi. Governor Koster’s line in September 2026: tender late 2026, works early 2027. Until there is a contractor on a awarded section, Medewi is a coast with today’s drive time. Underwrite today’s drive time. If the road arrives, it arrives as a change to a file that already worked, not as the thing the file required.

JPNN, 22 September 2026.

What to demand of a Tabanan site

The same three documents as a Canggu site, with less room for “everyone builds here.” A quieter market is where a wrong zone hides longer, because the neighbours have not yet been forced to regularise. Steel stud does not care which regency it stands in. The spatial plan does. Pair this note with the permit sequence before you pair it with a view.
