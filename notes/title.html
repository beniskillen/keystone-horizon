The certificate is the product.

Law 5 of 1960 keeps Hak Milik with Indonesian citizens. Everything sold to a foreign buyer is a structure around that fact. This page is a reading, not advice.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

The right that is not on offer

Hak Milik is the freehold right in the Basic Agrarian Law, and it is for Indonesian citizens. A foreign household does not become a freeholder because a local name is written on the certificate, or because a side letter says the name is only a convenience. That nominee pattern is the failure mode the market still dresses as ownership. A Pondok Wisata licence, which is about receiving guests, does not repair the certificate underneath it.

Buildings without the planning and permit file have been demolished. The 18 September call recorded that as a fact of the island, not as a metaphor. PP 16 of 2021 already lists demolition among the enforcement tools. Title and permit fail in different offices. They fail the same investor.

The words KHC already uses in public

A PT PMA, the foreign-investment company, and a lease put through an Indonesian notary. The company is the entity that can hold a right to build. The notarial deed is the instrument a court can read. What those two protect, and what they do not, waits on a legal memo that is still owed. Until that memo is signed, this site will not call the structure risk-free, tax-free, or invisible to Australian law.

Australian outreach has its own gate. The working assumption in the command centre is wholesale or sophisticated investors, the kind of test in section 708 of the Corporations Act, and it is not confirmed in writing by the client yet. A research note is not an offer document. If you cannot be shown that path, you should not be shown a term sheet.

What to ask for on paper

  • The right the company holds, named, and the term of the lease.
  • The notary, and the deed number, not a screenshot of a chat.
  • Who the shareholders are, and who can sell.
  • A written refusal of any nominee arrangement, in the same file as the picture of the pool.

Christopher is not your lawyer. Neither is this page. The point of the page is to stop the conversation starting at the furniture.
