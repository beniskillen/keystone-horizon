The field is good at the launch.

Kiss is the comparison named on the 18 September call. Magnum is the full-cycle developer a buyer will meet next. Neither page is a KHC result.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

Kiss Developments

The homepage is written for Australian investors and it is clear. Projects that sell out on launch. A claim that 29% of investors are Australian. A claim that Bali welcomed 12 million tourists in 2025. A claim of 15% to 20% annual returns against 2–3% in traditional property markets, and a five-to-seven-year payback for off-plan buyers at The Pash and Giselle. Apartments from $260,000, luxury villas from $540,000, up to a month of personal use, and a line that Bali is number one on Airbnb in Asia. The Pash in Pererenan and Giselle in Seseh are marked sold out, with another Seseh release aimed at October 2026. The office line is Pererenan, Canggu.

Take the craft seriously. The page knows its reader, names its projects, and does not hide the holiday month. Then separate the craft from the evidence. The arrivals claim does not match BPS. The Australian yield claim sits under Cotality’s published gross yield. The return claim is a projection. The 29% investor share is their sentence, not a statistician’s. On the 18 September call, the operating contrast was simpler than any of those numbers: Kiss buys, designs, and hands the asset over. The investor then owns whatever the manager inherits.

kissgroup.co and the contact page.

Magnum Estate

PT Magnum Estate International, founded in 2019, based in Kerobokan, describes a full cycle: design, build, sell, and manage, eight developments and 575 residences across Canggu and Berawa, Sanur, Umalas, and Uluwatu. Public entry is often quoted from about $225,000, with project pages higher, and a published projected ROI band of about 9.5% to 12.3% that they label as projections. Named examples on their own pages include Magnum Resort Sanur, The Umalas Signature, and others, each with its own figure. They are explicit that you buy from the developer rather than a broker, and that an in-house team manages after purchase.

That is closer to a stay than a pure handover. It is still a whole-unit trade in established investment streets, led by a projected return. KHC does not adopt the band, and does not need to diminish the company to describe a different spec: steel stud, a capped build queue, Tabanan as the window rather than the already-famous streets, and a wholesale path rather than a public villa from a few hundred thousand.

The seam

The niche also contains buyer’s agencies, token platforms, and content machines. This note stays with the two developers a reader can check today. The question to put to all of them, including KHC, is the same. Who is on the certificate the building needs before it can earn, and is that the same name that took the money. A sold-out launch answers a sales question. It does not answer year five.
