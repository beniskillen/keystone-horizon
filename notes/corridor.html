Look · Places

The corridor is a set of jobs, not a brand.

South Bali is not one market with one guest. The map on this site is a file of places. This note is what each place is being asked to do.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

The established coast

Canggu, Berawa, Pererenan, and Seseh are the loud end. They take the short-stay guest who wants a pool, a cafe street, and a driver to the rest of the island. They are also where new supply has been easiest to photograph. Listing growth and occupancy pressure get talked about together here for a reason: the guest is real, and so is the next villa. Uluwatu and the Bukit sell the cliff and the dry season. The setback, the building approval, and the certificate of use decide whether the view can be let. Seminyak is the older street market, still in every address list, including the mismatch on KHC’s own public pages: the company page has said Tabanan, the Carrd has said Seminyak. A place name is not a title.

Inland, and the west

Ubud is a different stay. The guest is there for the ridge and the terrace, not for a beach club. The spatial plan still decides whether a house is allowed to be a business. Tabanan, including the Jatiluwih side and the coast toward Medewi, is the window named in the strategy: inside the island’s demand, outside the most picked-over streets. That is a location thesis. It becomes a land thesis only when a specific contour has a zone, a notarial description, and a price you can tie to a document. Until then, “cheaper than Canggu” is a direction of travel, not a measured discount. The internal per-square-metre range stays off this note.

North, and the islands beside

North Bali and Lombok sit on the scouting list, not in the build queue. An airport rumour or a second-island story is a research subject. It is not a reason to skip KKPR. The cohort notes are where that scouting is supposed to land, once the wording is confirmed. The south corridor is the part dense enough to compare segment against segment: street villa, cliff villa, inland stay, west-coast hold.

Infrastructure that might change the drive times is in the status note. None of it turns five places into one yield.
