Look · Households

The house is already the portfolio.

Most Australian household wealth is the dwelling. The offshore question shows up when that dwelling stops feeling like a plan. The legal gate shows up immediately after.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

What the household already owns

Cotality’s August 2026 chart pack described the housing market as $12.4 trillion across 11.5 million dwellings and 56.8% of household wealth. The ABS June quarter print, a later and mean-based cut, put the stock at $12.7 trillion and the mean dwelling at $1.1 million. Use either one and the point holds: the typical store of wealth is a house in a city, financed, lived in, and recently off its peak in Sydney and Melbourne while still elevated in Perth, Brisbane, and Adelaide.

The published gross rental yield in that Cotality pack was 3.7% in July, with rents rising faster than wages. A household that lives in the dwelling does not receive that yield. A household that holds a second dwelling receives it gross, then pays interest, maintenance, agent fees, and vacancies. The frustration in the strategy file is real: domestic residential can be a large asset with a thin cash result. That frustration is not a licence to buy the next story that quotes a teen return.

The question, stated plainly

An everyday allocator is usually asking three things. Can a slice of capital sit somewhere that is not the same Australian housing cycle. Can it be owned in a way a lawyer can read. Can someone still be there when the tap leaks in year four. Bali enters the conversation because the household has already holidayed there, because friends have sent a launch, and because Australia’s gross yield is a small number next to a brochure.

Australia’s share of foreign arrivals, 23.44% in 2025, explains the friends. It does not explain the title. Freehold, Hak Milik, is an Indonesian-citizen right under the Basic Agrarian Law. A name on a certificate that is not the investor’s name is the pattern this market still sells around. The household version of the mistake is treating a holiday month and a furniture package as ownership.

Who this note is not recruiting

KHC’s path is written for people who can be shown a structure, a build method, and a stay, and who fit the wholesale gate once that gate is confirmed in writing. It is not a savings-account product, and it is not a second holiday house with a yield glued on. If the only ticket that works is “my own villa, my own month, a nominee if needed,” this file will disappoint on purpose. The longer answer for an operating business, as distinct from a household, is the next note.
