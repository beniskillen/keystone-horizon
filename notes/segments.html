Say which asset you are buying.

“Bali property” is five trades wearing one postcard. The risk, the operator, and the exit change when the asset changes.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

Land

A contour, a zone, a right someone can hold. Foreign buyers do not pick up Hak Milik. The trade is usually a lease, or a company that can hold a right to build, or a structure a salesperson would rather not diagram. Land is where the KKPR either exists or the rest of the dream is theatre. Price talk in this segment is local and lumpy. Bank Indonesia’s index does not measure a Pererenan rice field.

The whole villa

This is the product Kiss and a long list of launch developers sell to Australians: an apartment from a few hundred thousand, a villa higher, furniture, a month of personal use, a payback printed in years. Kiss’s homepage puts apartments from $260,000 and luxury villas from $540,000, with up to one month of use. Magnum’s public range starts near $225,000 and, on individual projects, much higher. You own a specific room. You also own the building’s mistakes, the licence, and the manager you get after completion unless the developer’s own team stays.

kissgroup.co. Magnum Estate, about.

The hotel, and the short-stay book

Star hotels are a BPS series. Bali’s 2025 occupancy, 61.02%, led the country. That is a formal room stock with a star rating. Short-stay villas and apartments listed like homes are a platform series. Airbtics, as reported by Villa Finder Magazine, had median occupancy of 65% across Bali listings from November 2024 to October 2025, average annual revenue about IDR 326 million a listing, and monthly revenue down 6.13% on the prior year while arrivals were rising. More guests and a softer listing dollar can be true together. A hotel rate card and a villa ADR should not be swapped to make a point.

Company equity

A PT PMA is a foreign-investment company. Capital can sit in the company that holds the project, rather than in a single front door. That is a share of a business: land rights the company can hold, a build, a management line, an exit written at entry. It is also the highest-sensitivity rung in KHC’s own papers, and it waits on the legal memo before this site describes what the share protects. It is not a token, and it is not a furniture package.

How to choose a segment before a price

If you want a month in a house, you are in the whole-villa trade, and you should underwrite the operator who stays. If you want a measured public market, you are closer to hotels and to Australian dwellings, both of which publish. If you want the stack, you are in company equity plus a build method, and you should be able to name the zone. Mixing the segments is how a land story, a hotel occupancy, and a villa payback end up in one paragraph.
