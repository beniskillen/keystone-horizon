Decide · Fit

A stalled allocator, not a holiday.

The reader in the strategy is about 45 to 60, with capital from a business or a fund that can do the work, already uneasy with domestic residential, and already stopped once at a nominee.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

The person

They have looked at a whole Bali villa and stalled on title. Or they have looked at a fund, a REIT, or a token and could not tell what they owned. The objections are ordinary and correct: is it legal, can I exit, who is accountable. They do not need another sunrise. They need a named operator and a file that can be read by their own adviser.

Two doors, same standard. A household whose wealth is the Australian dwelling, looking offshore because the gross yield is thin and the 2026 correction is uneven. A business owner placing retained earnings, who already knows what a bad handover costs. Both get the same sequence. Neither gets a retail product, and neither gets a return printed as if the claims register had cleared it.

Who is outside the file

A lifestyle buyer whose decision is the month of use. Someone whose only ticket works if the asset is a token. Someone who needs the investment to be simple in the way a listed share is simple. Bali company equity is not simple. The permits are sequential. The lease ends. The steel queue is short. If those sentences feel like obstacles rather than the product, the product is somewhere else, and Kiss’s holiday month may be the more honest fit for that reader.

The gate

Wholesale handling is the working rule for Australian outreach, and it is not yet confirmed in a client letter. Until it is, these notes stay educational. They describe mechanism and corridor. They do not invite the general public to subscribe for a security. If you are unsure whether you are inside that gate, that question comes before any allocation, and it is a question for a licensed adviser, not for a founder’s enthusiasm.
