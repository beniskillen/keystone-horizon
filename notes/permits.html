Use is a certificate, not a key.

A building can look finished and still be illegal to build, illegal to use, or sitting on land that was never zoned for the activity. The three documents are the product.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

KKPR, before anyone draws a plan

Before a business licence treats a site as real, the spatial check has to match the plan. Bali’s RDTR and RTRW are what that check is read against. A beautiful contour in the wrong zone is how a building later meets an enforcement notice. Source the land that is allowed to be the building. The homepage leads with place for this reason.

PBG, before the steel is ordered

Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung replaced the old IMB. Under PP 16 of 2021 it is the approval to build against the technical standards. The review is where coverage, floor area, green area, setbacks, and structure, including seismic design, get tested. For an operator, the company that will live with the drawing should be the company that builds it. Keystone Kayra is that build arm. A builder hired after the sale can treat the PBG as someone else’s document.

SLF, before the building may earn

Sertifikat Laik Fungsi is the certificate that the building is fit to be used. A PBG is not an SLF. A handover photograph is not an SLF. The certificate has to be renewed. This is the moment “stay” stops being a hospitality word. The handover model leaves the investor to obtain, renew, and defend that certificate after the builder has gone.

The order is the method

Source, structure, permit, specification, function, stay. Skip one and the arrival number does not save the asset. Illegal builds on the island have been taken down. That sentence is doing more work than any infrastructure rumour. The mistakes page collects the ways a licence for guests gets substituted for these three. This note is only the sequence.
