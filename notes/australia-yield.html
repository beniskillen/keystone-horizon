Look · Australia

Thin cash, large asset.

The dwelling is most of household wealth. The published gross yield is a small number. The 2026 correction did not turn it into a plan.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

Yield

Cotality’s August 2026 housing chart pack, discussing July, held annual rental growth at 5.9%, ahead of wage growth of 3.3%, and put the gross rental yield at 3.7%. Gross means before interest, insurance, rates, maintenance, and the weeks a second dwelling sits empty. An owner-occupier does not receive it at all. A marketing line that says Australian property pays 2–3% is harsher than this index. A marketing line that says it pays a comfortable income is kinder than the index. The index is the one to start from.

Cotality, Monthly Housing Chart Pack, August 2026.

Value

By August 2026 the national Home Value Index was 2.7% above a year earlier and 3.6% below the March peak, after five monthly falls, the last of them 0.9%. Sydney and Melbourne were down on the year. Brisbane, Adelaide, and Perth were still up on the year and down in the month. Ninety-three percent of capital-city suburbs recorded a winter value fall, on Cotality’s count. The ABS mean price in the June quarter was $1.1 million, down 0.7% in the quarter, inside a $12.7 trillion stock.

Cotality, 2026 downturn note. Westpac IQ, 1 September 2026.

Why this is the comparison that matters

Not because Bali’s official house index is racing ahead. It is not. Denpasar’s primary index gained about one percent in the year to the fourth quarter of 2025. The comparison matters because Australian capital is concentrated in an asset class that publishes a modest gross yield and, in 2026, a broadening price fall after a long rise. People look offshore from that position. The honest next step is a different asset with a readable title, not a larger percent from a different planet. The price note keeps the two indexes apart on purpose.
