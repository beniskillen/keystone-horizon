The corridor, before a product.

01 · Look A guest count is not a yield.

Direct foreign arrivals to Bali were 6.95 million in 2025, the highest year in the BPS series, with Australia about a quarter of them. The note is the monthly count, the 2019 comparison, and why a number from the airport is not a yield on a villa.

02 · Look Faster growth is a place, not a percent you can buy.

Bali’s provincial economy grew 5.82% in 2025. Australia grew 2.6% through the year to December. Rooms and food ran hotter than the official new-house index, which barely moved.
