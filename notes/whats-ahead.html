A groundbreaking is not a train.

Five projects get stapled to sales lines. The status is the content. Buy the asset that can operate under the rules that exist now.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

Operating

Bali Gapura Marina, inside the Bali Maritime Tourism Hub at Benoa, is the one a visitor can stand on. Pelindo opened Dock B and Dock C in July 2026, with capacity quoted at 75 yachts, including vessels to 200 feet, and a fuller marina target of 180 yachts in 2027. That is a harbour investment with a date and an operator. It is not a reason that a villa in Tabanan has changed price.

Pelindo, via IDX Channel, 27 July 2026.

Designated, and not yet a financial centre

KEK Kura Kura Bali is Government Regulation 23 of 2023. About 498 hectares on Serangan, south Denpasar. The SEZ National Council verified operational readiness in March 2024. The zone is framed for tourism, culture, and the creative economy, with a tax-holiday structure around that purpose. A special economic zone is not, by itself, an international financial-centre licence.

The financial-centre idea is a separate sentence. Coordinating Minister Airlangga has said the government is finishing rules for a financial-sector SEZ in Bali, with Kura Kura, Sanur, and sites near Benoa among the locations being looked at. As of May 2026 it had not been designated. “Under preparation” is the honest label.

PP 23/2023. Coordinating Ministry, via kek.go.id.

Announced, and not built

The urban subway had a first stone in September 2024, under PT Sarana Bali Dwipa Jaya. A later phase is described as Cemagi–Canggu–Mengwi. In February 2026 the Bali Post reported the Kuta site still had no follow-on works, and the provincial legislature had not been given a validated progress file. A train that is not in the ground does not shorten a drive.

The Gilimanuk–Mengwi toll was re-scoped. Gilimanuk–Pekutatan is no longer a toll. What remains is about 42 kilometres, Pekutatan to Mengwi. In September 2026 the governor’s line was tender late 2026, physical works early 2027. Medewi and the west coast feel that road if it is built. They do not feel it in a render.

Bali Post, 9 February 2026. Governor Koster, via JPNN, 22 September 2026.

How to hold all five

Rank them by what exists: marina open, zone designated, financial centre not designated, subway without follow-on works, toll still a tender. Then ask whether the asset you are shown can take a guest if none of the unbuilt items arrive. If the answer depends on the train, the file is a bet on a project, not a stay.
