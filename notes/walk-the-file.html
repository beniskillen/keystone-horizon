Decide · The call

Walk the file before you wire the capital.

The call is a test of names and documents. It is not a webinar recap, and it is not a close.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

Bring a short list

  • The place. Tabanan, Seminyak, or a specific banjar. One address.
  • The right the company will hold, and the notary who will see it.
  • Which of KKPR, PBG, and SLF exist for the site in front of you.
  • The wall section, the factory, and what “about four” means this year.
  • Who manages, and which of the two public fee lines is the current one.
  • How an exit works at the end of the lease, in writing.
  • What your own adviser still has to clear under Australian law.

Leave the IRR at home until he can show the model with sources. If the answer to a document question is a render, the call has drifted back into the genre these notes are trying to leave.

What he should be able to say without a slide

That freehold is not the offer. That Kayra is the build. That the steel queue caps the year. That illegal builds have been demolished, so the file is not decorative. That Kiss is the handover comparison, not a villain in a story. That the arrivals record is BPS, and the land CAGR on the areas page is not. A founder who can say those sentences is more useful than a founder who can say a percent.

Book the walk from the walk page. Treat the form as a draft. Nothing on this site is an application for securities.
