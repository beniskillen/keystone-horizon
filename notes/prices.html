Look · Prices

Draw the series you can name.

Australia has a monthly dwelling-value index. Denpasar has a quarterly index of new houses sold by developers. Put them on one page. Do not pretend they are one market.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

What Australia did by August 2026

Cotality’s national Home Value Index fell 0.9% in August 2026, the fifth monthly fall in a row. Westpac’s reading of that release put annual growth at 2.7%, and the level 3.6% under the March 2026 peak. The same table has Sydney down 4.6% on the year and 1.4% in the month, Melbourne down 4.7% on the year, Brisbane still up 10.8% on the year while falling 1.0% in August, Adelaide up 8.6%, Perth up 15.6%. A national median dwelling value of $912,885 was reported with that release. Combined capitals were $990,394.

The ABS is a different cut. In the June quarter 2026 the total value of residential dwellings fell 0.3% to $12.7 trillion. The mean price fell 0.7% to $1.1 million. Mean price and median value are not interchangeable, and a stock total moves when new dwellings are added, not only when prices move. From the ABS table itself, the June quarter 2026 stock ($12,689 billion) sits above the June quarter 2025 stock ($11,697 billion). That is the dwelling pile. It is not a suburb.

Westpac IQ, 1 September 2026, on Cotality. Medians: Broker Daily, citing Cotality, 1 September 2026. Stock and mean: ABS, June quarter 2026.

What Denpasar’s official index did

Bank Indonesia’s residential property price survey covers the primary market: new houses, in eighteen cities, index 2018 = 100. It is not a Canggu land print and it is not a leasehold villa. In that survey the Denpasar total index was 102.28 in the fourth quarter of 2022, 102.71 a year later, 104.55 in the fourth quarter of 2024, 105.66 in the fourth quarter of 2025, and 106.04 in the second quarter of 2026. From the fourth quarter of 2024 to the fourth quarter of 2025 that is 1.06%, the same pace the Bali representative office reported for the province. The national index in the second quarter of 2026 was 110.89, up 0.69% on the year.

Index levels: Bank Indonesia, Survei Harga Properti Residensial, Tw II 2026, Tabel 1 and the Denpasar rows of Tabel 2. National pace: Bank Indonesia news release, 8 July 2026.

The chart people ask for, and the one that exists

A fair overlay would be “Australian house prices versus Bali villa prices, same method, same years.” That overlay does not exist in the official statistics. Cotality covers established Australian dwellings. Bank Indonesia covers new developer houses in Denpasar, small, medium, and large, and the large-house index there was only 104.93 by the second quarter of 2026. Eight years after the base, Denpasar’s official new-house index is a few points above 100. Australia’s dwelling market, over the same era, has been a boom, a 2022 break, another rise, and a 2026 correction that is still uneven by city.

The emergence people feel in Canggu, Pererenan, and Uluwatu is a leasehold and land trade sitting beside a tourism boom. It is real as a market. It is not this index. Treat any CAGR printed beside a rice-terrace photograph as a private file until the compiler, the dates, and the sample are on the page. The areas page on this site still carries an internal land file. These notes do not use it.
