Decide · Offers

Start at the document, not the percent.

The percent is the last line that should move you. The first lines are the right, the zone, the approval, the wall, and the name that stays.

Research note, written for the file. Not an offer, not a forecast, and not a KHC return. Every figure below is someone else’s published statistic, with the source named. Australian wholesale rules are a gate, not a paragraph.

A sequence that fits on one card

  1. What is being sold: land, a whole villa, a hotel room, a listing business, or shares in a company.
  2. What right is held, by whom, for how many years, and in which deed.
  3. Whether any nominee is in the structure. If yes, stop.
  4. KKPR, PBG, SLF: which exist now, which are promised, who applies.
  5. The wall, in a section, with a supplier. Not a render.
  6. Who manages, on what share, and who pays for the year-five work.
  7. Only then the model: occupancy, rate, costs, and what happens if arrivals are flat.

Kiss will pass some of these in public and fail others, because the public offer is a designed villa with a payback and a holiday month. Magnum will pass the “one company manages” test more easily and still ask you to accept a projected ROI as the headline. A token platform will pass the ticket-size test and fail the “what do you own” test. KHC should be held to the same card. If we cannot show the deed, the zone, and the stud, the homepage is ahead of the file.

Percents that are allowed in the room

Published ones, labelled. Bali GRDP 5.82% in 2025. Australian GDP 2.6% through the year to December 2025. Denpasar’s official new-house index up about 1% a year, not 20. Cotality national dwelling values up 2.7% in the year to August 2026, with Sydney and Melbourne down on that year. Star-hotel occupancy 61.02%. Listing revenue down 6.13% in the Airbtics year while arrivals rose. A project’s projected ROI, if shown, stays in the operator’s voice. It does not become “the market.”

Percents that wait

Any KHC IRR, cash yield, multiple, or land-cost discount. They live in an internal model and they are not cleared. A note that smuggles them in as “illustrative” is still a projection. The walk is a conversation about the stack. It is not a term sheet.
